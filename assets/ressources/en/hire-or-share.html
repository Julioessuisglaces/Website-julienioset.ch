What the numbers don’t say

Cost isn’t the only factor. Here is when each option is the right one.

Hire if

  • You already have three or more marketing people to manage every day.
  • Your market demands a daily presence: continuous launches, trade fairs, a distribution network to support.
  • Your need stays above the threshold shown above.

Share if

  • You lack strategy, not hands: positioning, value arguments, priorities.
  • Marketing currently rests on you, on a junior profile or on an agency with no one in charge.
  • You want a commitment you can stop if results don’t follow.
